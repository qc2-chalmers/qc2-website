Bragi at the Munich Quantum Software Forum

October 14-15, 2026

Our software team travelled to the Munich Quantum Software Forum to present Bragi, the calibration toolkit we develop to characterize and operate the superconducting quantum processors made in our lab. We gave a short pitch on stage and presented a poster during the poster session.

Representing QC2 were Axel Andersson, Matteo Robbiati, Joel Sandås and Michele Faucci Giannelli, on behalf of the whole QC2 software team.

Meeting the community

The forum was a great networking opportunity. Beyond presenting our work, we talked with many people from the quantum software community and caught up with our collaborators on international projects such as EuroQSS and OpenSuperQPlus, as well as with long-standing partners like Qblox. It was also the chance to establish new connections with other groups, such as the authors of OQTOPUS.

The poster

The poster gives an overview of Bragi: its modular monorepo, the CZ calibration pipeline, a readout crosstalk characterization and the interactive GUI.
